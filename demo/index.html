Contoso Tasks on your phone

The app has the same feedback board, inside the app. It is the ArghBox mobile page in a WebView: one address, no sign-in, no native screens to build. Votes and ideas from the app and the website land in the same box.

  1. In the phone, tap Send feedback.
  2. Vote, add an idea, then close the board.
  3. Watch the messages the app receives.

Messages to the app

What the app's bridge receives, newest first: open, vote, add (an entry was sent), link (open it in the system browser) and close (dismiss the WebView). Never anything the visitor typed.

Questions

Is Contoso Tasks a real product?

No. It is a made-up site, so ArghBox has something realistic to sit on.

Where does my feedback go?

Into the ArghBox on this site. New ideas wait until the owner publishes them, which you can do yourself on the admin page.

Do I need an account to vote?

No. Nobody signs in, and a quiet human check keeps bots out.
