Tiny map tools for people who just need a pin.

Drop pins, draw a route, share a link. No account, no tracking, no 40 MB app. Pocket Maps is a made-up site that shows how ArghBox looks on a real page.

Drop a pin

Tap the map, name the place, copy the link. Friends see exactly the spot you meant.

Draw a route

Click a few points and get the distance and a rough walking time. Great for a Sunday loop.

Works offline

Save an area before you leave. Your pins stay on your device, not on our servers.

Questions

Is Pocket Maps a real product?

No. It is a pretend site so the ArghBox widget has something realistic to sit on.

Where does the feedback go?

Into a SQLite file on this dev server. In production, you triage it with your own AI assistant.

Do I need an account to vote?

No. A tiny Turnstile check keeps bots out and nobody has to sign in.
