# Code of Conduct

ArghBox follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Please read it: it applies to everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where the project is represented.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, give and accept feedback gracefully, and do not harass, insult or discriminate. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads and block people who do not follow these rules.

## Reporting a problem

To report behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct, use GitHub's private reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say in the title that it is a conduct report. This reaches the maintainers privately. We will review it fairly and keep the reporter's details confidential.

Enforcement follows the guidelines in the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
