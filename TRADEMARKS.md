# Trademarks

ArghBox™ and the ArghBox logo (the red box with the yellow "argh" burst) are trademarks of BinaryBanana LLC.

The code stays MIT licensed. You can use, copy, change, fork, host and sell it, as the [licence](LICENSE) says. The licence covers the code. It does not cover the name and the logo, because they tell people where something comes from. This page says how you can use them. The goal is simple: anyone who sees "ArghBox" should get the real ArghBox or something that is clearly about it.

## Fine without asking

- **Run ArghBox on your site** and keep the name and the credit link in the dialog. That includes your own settings, colours, wording, translations and small fixes.
- **Say what your project uses or works with:** "powered by ArghBox", "uses ArghBox", "works with ArghBox", "a Jira exporter for ArghBox".
- **Write about it:** articles, reviews, videos, talks, tutorials and courses.
- **Use the logo to point at the project:** link to arghbox.com or this repository with the files in [art/](art/), without changing them.
- **Publish a fork under its own name**, and say it is "based on ArghBox" or "a fork of ArghBox".

## Please ask first

- **Calling a changed version "ArghBox"** when you publish it for others, as a fork, a package, a plugin or a distribution. Give it its own name instead.
- **Offering a hosted service or a paid product named ArghBox.** You can host or sell the code under your own name.
- **Using "ArghBox" or a lookalike** in the name of a product, company, package, domain, app or social account. "Jira exporter for ArghBox" is fine. "ArghBox Pro", "ArghBox Cloud" or arghbox-hosting.com is not.
- **Using the logo, or something close to it,** as the logo of your own product.
- **Suggesting that BinaryBanana LLC made, approves or supports** your product when it does not.

## How to ask

Use the Feedback tab on [arghbox.com](https://arghbox.com/) and start with "Trademark:". Say what you want to call your project and how it relates to ArghBox. The answer appears on your entry under "Your arghs", in the browser you sent it from.

This policy may change as the project grows. It never takes back anything the MIT licence gives you for the code.
